# A Song For Life · 生き甲斐

Named after the song that started it all - "A Song for the Life", written by Rodney Crowell, though it's the Waterboys' version off _Karma to Burn_ that I keep coming back to. There's a line in it about learning how to listen, to a sound like the sun going down, that has kept my feet on the ground for years. The words belong to the people who wrote and sang them, but you can [read the full lyrics on Genius](https://genius.com/The-waterboys-a-song-for-the-life-lyrics).

### The Starting Point...
Music has been my thing since I was ten - the day I bought my first "music centre" with money earned climbing across the rooftops of Northern Ireland. The quest to find the next sound hasn't stopped since.

After my first 90-in-90 I started collecting lyrics. Printed them out, bound them with a bull-clip. It was barely a book, but it did what books do - it brought me back to myself when I needed a quiet moment.

This is an attempt to do something with that _pile_.
