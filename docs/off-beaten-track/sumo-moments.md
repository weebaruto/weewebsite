# Finding Sumo Moments · 相撲

:::tip[TL;DR]
A [wee](https://wrookieschu.com/2025/07/02/waynes-word-wee/) looksee into the how and where you can experience some "Sumo Moments" either through an official tournament or the not so public events.
:::

## Official

<Spot title="15 Day Tournaments" href="https://www.sumo.or.jp/EnTicket/year_schedule/" kind="Tournament">

Six 15 day tournaments a year, 3 in Tokyo, 1 in Osaka, Nagoya and Fukuoka. Tickets are like fairy dust, but you never know, collect a bunch of friends and try getting the lottery tickets!

</Spot>

<Spot title="Retirement Ceremony" href="https://www.japantimes.co.jp/sports/2019/01/17/sumo/sumo-101-retirement-ceremonies/" kind="Ceremony">

When a wrestler retires and he has reached a high rank there is usually a ceremony held in Ryokogu. It's unique, with a lot of ceremonies, and a day of demonstrations from the wrestlers ending with the hair cutting ceremony. Check out the Japanese events site - [https://www.sumo.or.jp/IrohaEventSchedule/](https://www.sumo.or.jp/IrohaEventSchedule/)

</Spot>

<Spot title="Special 1 Day Events" kind="Event">

There are special one-day tournaments held in Tokyo during the year, there is even a global kids tournament! Check out the Japanese events site - [https://www.sumo.or.jp/IrohaEventSchedule/](https://www.sumo.or.jp/IrohaEventSchedule/)

</Spot>

## Off The Beaten Track

<Spot title="Hono Ozumo at Yasukuni Shrine" href="https://www.visiting-japan.com/en/articles/events/e13cy-yasukuni-ozumo.htm" kind="Event">

Usually happens on a business day in early April and offers a whole day covering different aspects of Sumo, closing out with some matches. Some great photo opportunities and it's all free!

</Spot>

<Spot title="Dohyo Blessing Ceremony" href="https://sumowrestling.fandom.com/wiki/Dohyo_Matsuri" kind="Ceremony">

Open to the public (free) and happens the day before each of the 15 day tournaments. It starts at 10:00AM and you will have to check the news on the Japanese [Official Sumo Site](https://www.sumo.or.jp/IrohaEventSchedule/index/) for details beforehand.

</Spot>

<Spot title="Yokozuna Deliberation Council Soken" href="https://www.japantimes.co.jp/sports/2023/05/10/sumo/yokozuna-deliberation-council-return/" kind="Training">

A keiko (training) session before each 15 day tournament where the council reviews and ranks the wrestlers before the tournament so that they can create the [Banzuke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banzuke). Some are open to the public (free) you will have to check the news on the Japanese [Official Sumo Site](https://www.sumo.or.jp/IrohaEventSchedule/index/) for details beforehand.

</Spot>

<Spot title="Asageiko" href="https://blog.gaijinpot.com/how-to-watch-a-sumo-morning-training-session/" kind="Training">

Morning training sessions are conducted early morning at all stables and you can attend as part of a tour (fee) or by simplifying asking (free)

</Spot>

<Spot title="Regional Tours" href="https://www.sumo.or.jp/EnJungyo/" kind="Event">

Between the 15 day tournaments the wrestlers go on regional tours to where no tournament is held and conduct one-day special events. So, you could catch some sumo if you happen to be in the area at the same time.

</Spot>
